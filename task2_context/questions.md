# Questions for the Meridian Grid dossier

One numbered question per item. `run_questions.py` asks them in this order and saves the answers to
`outputs/answers.json`. Add, remove or edit items freely (keep the `N.` numbering at the start of each).

1. Which incident had the highest reconciled cost, and who was the on-call engineer for it?
2. Which vendor was flagged as a compliance risk in the Q3 audit, and what is its annual contract cost?
3. Which client received the "Firewall Rule Auto-Validator v2" tool, and what is that client's monthly revenue?
4. Who was Head of Security during the company's founding year, and how long did they hold the role?
5. Using the severity-weighting table in Section 6, what is the total severity-weighted cost of all incidents caused by "misconfigured firewall rule"?
6. According to Appendix B, what is the renewal date for the SentinelWatch SIEM contract, and who must approve it?
